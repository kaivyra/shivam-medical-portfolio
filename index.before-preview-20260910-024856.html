About

I'm an MBBS second-year student at Maharaja Suhel Dev Autonomous State Medical College, Bahraich, affiliated with Atal Bihari Vajpayee Medical University.

Right now, most of my time goes into building a solid foundation in the pre-clinical and para-clinical sciences — the kind of grounding that clinical years and, eventually, patient care will depend on.

Outside the syllabus, I'm drawn to medical research and to healthcare innovation: how evidence gets made, and how good ideas in medicine actually reach patients rather than staying in journals.
