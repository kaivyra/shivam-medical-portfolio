1. Not medical advice

Nothing on KAIVYRA is a diagnosis, treatment recommendation or substitute for a qualified health professional. Calculators and checklists are educational aids and have not been reviewed by a clinician. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

2. Third-party news and records

The live desk and search tools show headlines, short excerpts, dates and links taken from public sources such as the Press Information Bureau (Government of India), Europe PMC and ClinicalTrials.gov. Releases of the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) and the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) appear only when the Press Information Bureau carries them; KAIVYRA does not read ICMR or MoHFW feeds directly. The Press Information Bureau permits free reproduction of its content, provided it is reproduced accurately and the source is clearly acknowledged, so each item names its source. Copyright and other rights in that material remain with the original publisher. We show the minimum needed to identify an item, we do not alter the headline, we mark shortened excerpts, and we credit the source on each item.

Names of organisations appear only to say where an item comes from. No listed organisation reviews, sponsors, partners with or endorses KAIVYRA, and KAIVYRA does not speak for any of them. Logos and trademarks belong to their owners.

4. No independent verification

Items are collected automatically from the sources we list. We do not fact-check them. A source can correct, withdraw or change an item after we have shown it, and an item can be delayed or missing. Always open the original before relying on it.
