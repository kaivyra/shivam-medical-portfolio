Source-grounded research assistant

Ask a research question. KAIVYRA retrieves real publications, labels their study types, and shows verbatim excerpts from their abstracts with numbered citations. It does not write new medical claims.

How this works today. This tool uses keyword retrieval and rule-based extraction, not a language model. It cannot judge whether a claim is true, and it does not detect agreement or conflict between sources automatically. You read the excerpts and decide. Not medical advice.

Extractive summarizer Live

Paste an abstract or passage. The tool selects the sentences that best represent it and returns them unchanged. It does not rewrite, interpret or add information.
