Research pipeline (educational)

Drug trials are commonly described by phase: Phase 1 mainly studies safety and dosing in small groups; Phase 2 looks for early signs of effect and further safety; Phase 3 compares the intervention with standard care in larger groups; Phase 4 follows an approved product in wider use. Not every study fits a phase, and a registered trial is not evidence that an intervention works.
