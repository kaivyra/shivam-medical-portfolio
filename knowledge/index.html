    How the atlas and framework work together

    1. Choose where to look

    The Atlas organises medical research into 9 groups, 50 domains and 519 topics. It is a KAIVYRA-curated taxonomy, not MeSH and not an official classification.

    2. Choose how to look

    The Framework gives 15 lenses (the human, biology, mechanism, diagnosis, treatment, access, systems, evidence, ethics, the frontier) so an inquiry is not limited to one angle.

    3. Search and record

    Every topic and dimension builds a Europe PMC query you can read and edit. Coverage and notes are saved in your Workspace, in this browser only.
