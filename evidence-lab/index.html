Understand and compare the evidence

Compare studies side by side, work through an appraisal checklist, and learn what each study design can and cannot show. Nothing here is a systematic review, a meta-analysis or a clinical recommendation, and no confidence score is calculated.

Pick 2 to 4 papers from your saved papers. Find papers in Research and press “Save to workspace”.

Choose papers to compare

Answer for a paper you are reading. The tool lists the questions you answered “No” or “Unclear” as points to examine. It gives no score: the answers are your judgement, not a validated risk-of-bias tool. For formal appraisal use established instruments (for example Cochrane RoB 2, ROBINS-I, AMSTAR 2) with a trained reviewer.

Study designs are often ordered from systematic reviews and randomized trials down to case reports and laboratory studies. That ordering is a teaching convention. A well-run observational study can be more useful than a poorly run trial, and design alone never settles quality.

Conflicting evidence

KAIVYRA does not automatically decide whether studies agree. When results seem to conflict, compare the studies above. The notes under the comparison table flag structural reasons (design, population, timing, size) that often explain different findings.
